Local by design

PresenceFM runs on your Mac. You decide what leaves it, and when.

  • No account. No backend.

    PresenceFM never asks you to create an account or connect to its own server.

  • Credentials stay on your Mac.

    Your Last.fm credentials and session token are kept in an owner-only local file.

  • Private Mode, on your terms.

    Pause Discord presence and Last.fm updates for a while—or until you return.
